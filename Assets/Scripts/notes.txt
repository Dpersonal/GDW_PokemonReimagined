What needs to happen in a turn

battle start
1. switch player menu to empty
2. init pokemon teams internally
3. switch onscreen pokemon to active
4. switch player menu to fight/switch

player selects move

perform turn
1. switch player menu to empty
2. If player chose to switch:
	a. switch player menu to pokemon switch
	b. wait for player to choose pokemon
	a. switch active pokemon and change onscreen pokemon
3. If player chose a move:
	a. check what rng is required to perform move
	b. perform qtes to get rng 
	c. 1 extra qte in case of sleep/freeze (sleep is a turn count)
4. give rng and selected moves to battleManager
5. battlemanager performs turn using moves and rng and returns turn result in struct(?)
6. use data in turnresult struct to perform animations
7. if battle over, do appropriate battle over flow
8. start pre turn


paralysis & freeze: 25% chance to skip turn
